Testing

How I test every bot

Every bot runs through the same attacks in a private sandbox before it touches a live community.

SPAMMessage floodsCan it stop a flood without slowing normal chat?
JOINMass joinsAlt accounts join at once. Does it react fast enough?
NUKEChannel & role deletesDoes the anti-nuke layer stop it and log who did it?
LINKBad linksScam and invite links are caught and removed.
PERMPermission checksThe bot can act on raiders but not on staff.
FPFalse positivesInnocent members must never be punished by mistake.
MOD FLOWModeration flow testA test case runs through every step: detect, triage, action ladder, log, then appeal and tune.
BOT FLOWBot flow testSandbox, permissions, simulate, check. A fail goes back to tune and re-test, and only a pass goes live.
