Polo, a health AI app

A health AI app for structured wellness check-ins, with clinical safety rules built in from the start.

In progress · product design and build

What it does

  • Walks users through a structured wellness check-in.
  • Safety isn't bolted on later. The clinical guardrails are part of the design.
  • Keeps the scope small so the first version can be tested with real users.

How it's built

  1. IdeaPick a health problem that suits a repeatable, structured check-in.
  2. DesignMap the wellness workflows step by step.
  3. SafetySet the clinical guardrails before any prototype is built.
  4. PrototypeBuild an AI-assisted prototype quickly.
  5. TestCheck it with real users before going further.
  6. LaunchRelease only once the safety checks have passed.
